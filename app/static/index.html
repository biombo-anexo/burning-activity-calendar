How to read the numbers

Fire activity counts “cell-days”: the map is split into cells of 0.1° (about 11 km) and a cell counts once for each day a satellite saw at least one fire in it. It measures how widespread and persistent burning was, and it is comparable across the whole record.

Anomaly compares a month with the median of the same month in the other years: +100% means twice the usual activity. Where the usual activity is very low, percentages jump around and are shown faded.

Values before 2012 come from MODIS converted to the VIIRS scale; they are estimates.
